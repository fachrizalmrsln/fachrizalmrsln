### About

Android engineer with 5+ years building consumer apps used by millions. I lead the **Feed & Core Video Android team** at Tokopedia, where I designed the company's standard video playback platform — a standalone core library module now consumed by Feed, Feed Detail, Homepage, PDP, Search, and every other video-enabled surface in the app.

My focus is platform architecture, performance engineering, and growing the engineers around me.

**A few things I've shipped:**
- 🎬 Designed Tokopedia's reusable video platform — playback lifecycle, scrolling behavior, and interaction patterns standardized across product surfaces
- ⚡ Cut Feed page load from **5s → 1.5s** (p90) with an offline-first caching layer over GraphQL that removed redundant network calls
- ⏱️ Cut initial video start from **3s → under 1s** by prebuffering ahead of playback
- 📊 Built a performance observability framework making video startup latency, render time, and playback regressions visible platform-wide
- 👥 Provide technical direction for 5 engineers across architecture, strategy, and delivery
